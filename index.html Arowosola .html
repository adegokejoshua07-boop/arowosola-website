Our Core Services

Seven trades, one team.

We specialize in roof and wall insulation, ceiling works, wall screeding and painting, paint production, property maintenance, cleaning services and more.

/01

Roof Insulation

High-performance thermal and acoustic insulation for residential and commercial roofs, lowering energy costs and improving comfort.

/02

Wall Insulation

High-performance thermal and acoustic insulation for interior and exterior walls, cutting energy costs and keeping every room comfortable.

/03

Ceiling Works (Suspended Ceiling, Gypsum Boarding – POP)

Design, installation and finishing of suspended ceilings, gypsum boarding (POP) and fixed ceiling systems, for a clean and durable result.

/04

Wall Screeding and Painting

Careful wall screeding for a true, level surface, followed by expert painting with quality paint for a flawless, long-lasting finish.

Paint Production

In-house manufacturing of quality paints, emulsions, satins and texturisers, formulated for Nigeria's climate.

/06

Property Maintenance

Comprehensive maintenance to keep a property in top condition, including repairs, inspections and routine upkeep.

/07

Cleaning Services

Professional cleaning for residential and commercial spaces, ensuring a spotless and healthy environment.
